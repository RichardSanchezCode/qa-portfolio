- `TC-002-pago-tarjeta.png`: card payment confirmation using a test card.
- `TC-003-emision-poliza.png`: issuance confirmation with the anonymized identifier.

No original evidence was attached in the received material due to the entity's confidentiality.

## Privacy Review Before Publishing
- Hide names, emails, phone numbers, RFC tax IDs, addresses, and customer data.
- Hide real folios, internal identifiers, tokens, and private URLs.
- Do not show card numbers, expiration dates, or CVVs.
- Confirm that publishing each screenshot is authorized.
